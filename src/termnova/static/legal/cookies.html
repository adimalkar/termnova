Browser storage

Cookie Notice

Termnova currently uses only cookies that are necessary to authenticate users and protect the sign-in flow. It does not use advertising or behavioral-tracking cookies.

Effective September 18, 2026Version 1.0
Why there is no consent banner today.

Consent banners are not useful when a site sets only strictly necessary cookies. If analytics, personalization, or advertising technologies are introduced, Termnova will add the required choices before enabling them.

What Termnova does not currently use

The hosted application does not currently set cookies for advertising, cross-site behavioral profiling, social-media tracking, audience measurement, or non-essential personalization. Embedded service providers may receive ordinary network request data when your browser requests their resources; the public website currently avoids third-party analytics scripts.

Your controls

You can remove cookies through browser settings or sign out of Termnova. Blocking the cookies above will prevent or interrupt authenticated workspace use. Closing a tab does not necessarily end the session; use the in-product sign-out control when using a shared device.

Browser “Do Not Track” signals do not change current behavior because Termnova does not conduct cross-site advertising tracking. Legally required opt-out signals will be honored if a relevant practice is introduced.

Changes and questions

This notice will be updated before materially different browser-storage technology is enabled. Questions can be sent through the operator contact form. See the Privacy Notice for the broader handling of personal information.
