    Termnova — Beta AI Contract Intelligence, Knowledge Graph & Clause Comparison

    Termnova is a beta legal intelligence platform for teams evaluating agreement analysis, risk discovery, clause changes, and multi-contract relationships. AI output requires source review and is not legal advice.

    Core Capabilities & Features

    • Contract Studio & Hybrid RAG: Natural language query answering with reciprocal rank fusion (PostgreSQL FTS + dense vectors) and page-level source excerpts.
    • Document Map & Contract Knowledge Graph: AI-powered entity extraction mapping parent Master Services Agreements (MSAs), Statements of Work (SOWs), Amendments, counterparty entities, and governing jurisdictions with D3.js force layouts.
    • Clause Redline & Side-by-Side Diff: Semantic difference highlighting detecting modifications in financial terms, liability caps, indemnification clauses, and termination windows.
    • Document Vault & Vector Repository: Multi-format ingestion supporting PDF, DOCX, TXT, and Markdown files with pgvector indexing. Tenant isolation is not yet production-ready.
    • Current Guardrails: PII and API-secret redaction assist reviewers, but provider retention and training policies depend on deployment configuration.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is Termnova?

    Termnova is a beta contract intelligence project with hybrid retrieval, optional LangGraph flows, and PostgreSQL-backed contract relationships.

    How does Termnova protect confidential legal documents?

    The application can redact common PII and API-key patterns, but enterprise tenant isolation and universal zero-retention controls are not yet implemented. Review the configured model and storage provider policies before using confidential data.

    What contract types are supported?

    Termnova analyzes MSAs, SOWs, NDAs, Amendments, Addenda, Commercial Leases, Vendor Agreements, and Employment Contracts.
